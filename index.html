Guide to the schemes
Simple
Twelve fixed steps: 3 juz spaced ten apart, then 6 spaced five apart, then eight blocks of five (odd then even in each third), then the five-juz blocks, then each half. Easy to remember; no formula.
Anchored on juz 1
Step k recites k juz starting at juz 1 and spaced 30/k apart. Juz 1 is in every step and juz 30 only appears at step 30, so the repetition count runs from 30 down to 1.
Rotating start
Same spacing, but step k starts at juz k and wraps around 30. Repetition count per juz falls between 9 and 22.
Balanced offsets
Same spacing; each step's start is chosen so the running totals stay even. Every juz ends up recited 14–17 times. The offsets have no pattern, so read them off the page.
Capped runs
No juz stays in the rotation more than L steps in a row; it then rests G steps. Steps are filled from the least-recited juz, which produces consecutive blocks sweeping through the mushaf. The cap cannot hold past the point where a step needs more than 30·L/(L+G) juz.
Formula
For the three spaced schemes: juz = 1 + (offset + ⌊i·30/k⌋) mod 30, for i = 0 … k−1.
Graph
Rows are steps, columns are juz. A juz carried over in consecutive steps is drawn as one merged block with a green edge. Blue is its first appearance in the scheme; amber is a return after a gap; red marks a run that exceeds the cap.
