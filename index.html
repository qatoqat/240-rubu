240 Rubu’

Guide
Scoring
Tap a juz, then each rubu’: A clean · B hesitated · C opened the mushaf. Hold a step until every juz in it is clean.
Simple
Twelve fixed steps: 3 juz ten apart, 6 five apart, eight odd/even blocks of five, the two sets of five-juz blocks, then each half.
Anchored
Step k: k juz from juz 1, spaced 30/k. Juz 1 in every step; juz 30 only at step 30.
Rotating
Same spacing, step k starts at juz k. Each juz recited 9–22 times over 30 steps.
Balanced
Same spacing, start chosen per step to keep totals even: 14–17 per juz.
Capped
No juz runs more than L steps in a row, then rests G. Filled from the least-recited juz. Cannot hold past 30·L/(L+G) juz per step.
Formula
juz = 1 + (offset + ⌊i·30/k⌋) mod 30, i = 0 … k−1.
Chips
Blue: first time in this scheme. Amber: back after a gap. Red border: has a C. Corner: progress, red → yellow → green.
Clean
Mark the step clean once every juz in it is A, then move the slider on.
Graph
Rows steps, columns juz. Merged block = carried over (green edge); red edge = over the cap.
